Mar 2026 · Pair project · 85% module result

Earth–Moon three-body simulation

A Python model of a spacecraft moving under Earth and Moon gravity, with a fourth-order Runge-Kutta solver written from scratch in NumPy.

Module: Year 2 Mathematics and Computing, finished with 85%.

Read more
  • Derived the rotating-frame equations of motion for the 3D circular restricted three-body problem.
  • Compared four sets of initial conditions, two planar and two 3D.
  • Validated the solver by tracking the Jacobi constant, which should stay fixed. Relative drift ranged from about 10-14 to 10-8.
  • Analysed the results with 3D trajectory plots, phase portraits, zero-velocity contour maps and an FFT with a Hanning window.

What I learnt: our biggest error was on the orbit that came closest to Earth, because that's where the time step matters most. Checking something that should stay constant is a quick way to catch it.

  • Python
  • NumPy
  • Numerical methods
