# Site plan

## Goal
A recruiter in consulting, finance, quant or engineering should get the picture in 30 seconds:
an Imperial engineer heading into consulting and finance, with real quantitative depth, who has
run his own business since 2022. Anyone curious can open the project details for more.

## Design
Keeps the original layout and look of this site: dark background, teal accent, Inter with
JetBrains Mono labels, numbered section headings, a timeline for experience and cards for
projects. It's deliberately different from the CCST site (navy, gold and teal with Fraunces).
Motion is limited to the hero typing line and a gentle fade-in, and both switch off with
reduced motion.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** name, rotating "I ..." line, one-line positioning, headshot frame, buttons for Download CV, LinkedIn and Email.
2. **About:** four short first-person paragraphs, plus a stats card (First Class, 30+ students, J.P. Morgan 2027, scholarships).
3. **Experience:** J.P. Morgan (incoming), CCST, RAEng, BP, GEEP, Student Partner. Earlier internships get one line each.
4. **Projects:** NoVac, EV transmission, three-body simulation, MEMARO 2.0 (DMT), Formula Student crankshaft, press drivetrain, AI in wearables. Each card has an image frame, summary, role and a "Read more" drop-down.
5. **Education:** Imperial (results and modules) and A-Levels.
6. **Skills & learning:** engineering, programming, certifications, and an honest "what I'm learning now" list with levels.
7. **Beyond work:** mentoring, public speaking and competitions, interests.
8. **Contact:** email, LinkedIn, GitHub only.

## Rules followed
- Facts come from the CVs. Nothing invented. Missing photos are visible `[TODO]` frames.
- The GitHub practice repos (quant-desk, learning sites, poker simulator) are not featured.
- No phone number, address or date of birth. Family only appears in the CCST origin line.
